About Me

Hi! I'm Jessie, the woman behind the Crocheting Captain. Crocheting started out as a fun hobby for me, but I quickly learned that it helps me in my day to day life, too. I have ADHD, and keeping my hands busy iwth a crochet project helps me focus when I'm in class, in a meeting, or even just watching tv.

Right now, I mostly make scarves and blankets. There's actually a pretty simple reason for it: I am still learning how to do the magic circle. Learning a new stich isn't a problem for me, but I find the magic circle a bit challenging. I am slowly getting better at it, though.

Once I master the magic circle and get more comfortable with it, I plan on branching out and adding more kinds of crochet items to the shop. For now, I am taking my time, learning as I go, and getting better with every project. I'd rather make things I am proud of than rush myself just because I feel like I should know how to make everything already.

When I'm not crocheting, I'm usually reading, gaming, cosplaying, working on something for my software development degree, or making tiktoks. I've always loved creative hobbies, so The Crocheting Captain gives me a place to share one of them while continuing to learn and improve my skills.
