Commission a Custom Crochet Piece

Looking for something custom-made for you? I would love to help make your idea a reality! Whatever you are looking for, a custom made cozy, a scarf in your favorite colors, or another custom crochet creation, I would love to hear your idea.

Each commission is carefully handmade and every piece is unique.

Current Commission Types

  • Scarves
  • Blankets
  • Custom color requests
  • Gift commissions

I will add more commission options in the future as my confidence in my skills as a crocheter grows.

The Commission Process

  1. Fill out the commission request form below.
  2. I'll review your request.
  3. We'll discuss details, colors, size, and pricing.
  4. Once everything is finalized, I'll begin creating your project.
  5. I'll keep you updated throughout the process.

Pricing Information

Pricing depends on several factors, including:

  • Project size
  • Materials required
  • Complexity
  • Time needed to complete the project

A price estimate will be provided before any work begins.

Please Keep in Mind

  • All items are handmade.
  • Completion times vary depending on project size and current workload.
  • Colors may vary slightly depending on yarn availability.
  • I reserve the right to decline commission requests.
